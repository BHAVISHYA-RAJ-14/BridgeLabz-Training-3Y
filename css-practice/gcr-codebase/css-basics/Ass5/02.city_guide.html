Welcome to Jaipur

Patna is the capital city of Bihar, India. It is famous for its history, culture, education, and connection to the River Ganga.

History of Patna

Patna is one of the oldest continuously inhabited cities in the world. It was known as Pataliputra and was an important centre during the Maurya and Gupta empires.

Famous Places

Golghar

A historic granary and one of the most recognised landmarks in Patna.

Patna Sahib Gurudwara

A significant religious site and the birthplace of Guru Gobind Singh Ji.

Bihar Museum

A modern museum that showcases the art, history, and culture of Bihar.
